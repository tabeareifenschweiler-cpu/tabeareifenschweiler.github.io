Hi, I’m Tabea!

A holistic designer passionate about the synergy between product design and visual communication. My goal is to think across disciplines and to develop projects that span across multiple dimensions.

My workflow shifts seamlessly between Rhino and Adobe, as I love the technical challenge just as much as the visual storytelling.

Whether it is precise 3D modeling or graphic staging: I thrive on diving deep into new topics and constantly pushing creative boundaries to find innovative solutions at the intersection of object and graphics.
